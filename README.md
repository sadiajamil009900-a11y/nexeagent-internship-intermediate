Factors That Improve Open Rate

Subject line personalization — using the recipient's name or company name can increase open rates by up to 26%.
Send time — Tuesday and Thursday mornings between 9am and 11am consistently outperform other times for B2B emails.
Preview text — the 40 to 90 characters shown after the subject line act as a second subject line. Always write it intentionally.
Sender name — emails from a person's name such as "Maryam from Nexe-Agent" get higher open rates than a company name alone.
List hygiene — removing unengaged subscribers every 60 days improves deliverability and open rates across the board.

Goal: Make potential B2B clients aware that Nexe-Agent exists and that it solves a problem they already have.
Channels:

Facebook posts and paid Facebook ads
LinkedIn content and outreach
